# The Mainstreaming of Subculture Gaming and Fandom Economics: From Screen to Japan Tourism

::: tip Key Insight
As gamers fatigued by pay-to-win MMORPGs embrace character-centric narrative subculture IPs, monetization has expanded beyond in-app purchases into **offline pop-up stores, character merchandise, and physical Japan pilgrimage tourism**.
:::

## 1. The Market Shift: From Minor Niche to Revenue Dominance
* Titles like *Goddess of Victory: NIKKE* and *Blue Archive* consistently secure #1 grossing ranks across global app stores.
* Transition from competitive stat-checking spend to narrative **Affection Economics**.

## 2. Cultural Synchronization and Physical Travel
* Visiting real-world Tokyo landmarks featured in in-game event backgrounds has become a primary travel motivator for Gen Z gamers.
