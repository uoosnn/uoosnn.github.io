# PlayX4, the Biggest Game Festival in the Capital Region, and I


Exciting news has arrived for gamers in the Seoul metropolitan area. One of South Korea's largest game shows, **'PlayX4'**, is making a comeback with a variety of new titles, and this year, I've finally decided to be there in person!

### News Summary

According to the Green Economic Newspaper, 'PlayX4,' the largest game exhibition in the Seoul metropolitan area, is about to open, and a large number of new games are scheduled to be unveiled at this event. From indie games to highly anticipated titles from major developers, the event is garnering high expectations from gamers by offering a wide range of attractions and hands-on opportunities.

### My Thoughts: The KINTEX Expedition is Finally Happening

To be honest, PlayX4 has always been an event I've heard about every year, thinking 'I should go someday,' but I always put it off. The location, KINTEX in Ilsan, was admittedly a bit of a burden in terms of distance.

But this year is different. I've resolved, "This is the year I'm definitely going!" It's about more than just getting a sneak peek at new games; I want to personally feel the passion of the developers and the vibrant energy of the gamers filling the venue. It's a long trip, but I'm confident that the value and enjoyment will be more than worth it. I'm already excited just thinking about sharing the vivid news I'll gather on the ground through my blog.

### Conclusion & Original Link

PlayX4 is a significant event that offers new fun for gamers and provides a valuable platform of opportunity for developers. If there are others who, like me, have been hesitant to visit, why not join in and experience the excitement of the event this year?
