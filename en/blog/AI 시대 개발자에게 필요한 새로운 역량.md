# New Competencies Required for Developers in the AI Era


The emergence of generative AI like ChatGPT is fundamentally changing the programming paradigm. In the past, the process of building code from scratch and implementing logic was crucial, but now AI can generate near-perfect code in just a few minutes.

Amidst this change, the role of developers and the competencies they need are also being redefined. What new skills are required for developers living in the AI era?

## 1. Focusing on 'What' to Build, Not 'How': Planning and Creativity

As AI significantly lowers the barrier to the act of writing code itself, programming is expanding beyond the domain of a few 'majors' into an area of 'general knowledge' that anyone can utilize. Now, contemplating **what to build (`What`)** has become far more important than the technical implementation methods (`How`).

Beyond simply churning out code, a fundamental mindset of defining problems and designing solutions from a system engineer's perspective has emerged as a core competency. The ability to plan and imagine what value to create using the powerful tool of AI becomes a developer's competitive edge.

## 2. The Power to Verify AI's Output: Solid Fundamentals and Security Knowledge

You cannot blindly trust the code generated by AI 100%. AI sometimes references non-existent libraries or suggests code containing critical security vulnerabilities.

Therefore, paradoxically, a fundamental knowledge of programming and an understanding of security have become even more important. It is ultimately the developer's responsibility to refine the convenient 'draft' suggested by AI into a safe and efficient 'final product' and take final ownership. Solid fundamentals are essential to always prioritize security and to be able to judge whether the generated code is easy to maintain.

## Conclusion: Beyond a Coder to a Creator

The era of generative AI signifies the end of the age of the 'Coder,' who simply writes code, and the dawn of the age of the true 'Creator,' who plans and realizes their own ideas.

AI is not a competitor that threatens developers' jobs, but a powerful partner that helps turn imagination into reality in the fastest way. If you have creative planning skills and the solid fundamentals to verify them, AI will become the ultimate tool to maximize a developer's productivity.
