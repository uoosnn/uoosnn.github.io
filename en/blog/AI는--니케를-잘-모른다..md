## Analysis: A Victorious Composition Forged from Strategy and Understanding

The composition the Commander used was **Anis / Crown / Neon / Cinderella / Mest**. Let's take a closer look at the role each Nikke played and how they synergized.

### 👑 Core Engine: Crown, Cinderella, and Mest

The core of this composition is the synergy between powerful buffers and the main damage dealer.

- **Crown (Burst 1)**: As one of the strongest supporters currently available, she maximizes the team's combat power on both offense and defense with overwhelming attack buffs, reload speed reduction, and shields.
- **Mest (Burst 2)**: A buffer who increases the entire team's Critical Damage, boasting the best synergy with a damage dealer who has a high innate Critical Rate.
- **Cinderella (Burst 3)**: A Liberation Nikke with immense firepower. She excels at dealing explosive damage to single bosses, serving as the key damage dealer for the Egovista fight.

> The synergy of these three is clear. Crown and Mest set the stage for Cinderella, and Cinderella, having received all the buffs, delivers the decisive blow. It's a stable yet powerful structure.

### 💎 Hidden Cards: Anis and Neon

What made this composition special was the inclusion of the SR Nikkes, Anis and Neon.

- **Anis / Neon (Shotgun)**: These two were likely included for strategic purposes, such as destroying specific parts or rapidly generating the Burst Gauge. Shotguns fill the Burst Gauge very quickly at close range, which can smooth out skill cycles and turn the tide of battle in your favor.

The fact that a clear was achieved by using SR Nikkes instead of high-performance SSRs is a result that could only be made possible by a deep understanding of the characters and outstanding control skills.

## Other Powerful Recommended Comps for Egovista

Besides the Commander's unique composition, there are many other powerful and stable teams that can take on Egovista. Here are a few representative examples.

This is the most widely used composition by Commanders, proven for both its stability and firepower.

- **Composition**: Liter / Blanc / Modernia / Red Hood / Noir
- **Strengths**:

  - **Modernia & Red Hood**: A top-tier DPS lineup that needs no introduction.

### 2. The 'Alice' One-Shot Comp (A High-Investment, Nuke Composition)

This composition requires high investment, but when successful, it provides the satisfaction of melting the boss in an instant.

- **Composition**: Liter / Blanc / Alice / Maxwell / Noir
- **Strengths**:
  - **Alice**: Maximizes her charge speed to fire lasers like a machine gun.
  - **Maxwell**: A key component who directly amplifies Alice's charge speed and attack power.

### 3. The Stability-Focused Comp (A Composition for Surviving with Heals and Defense)

When battles drag on or you're having trouble with boss patterns, this composition focuses on tenaciously surviving to secure the clear.

- **Composition**: Liter / Centi / Modernia / Rapunzel / Scarlet or Red Hood
- **Strengths**:

  - **Centi**: Provides shields and rapid Burst Gauge generation to ensure smooth skill cycles.

### 4. The Close-Range Shotgun Comp (An Aggressive Brawling Composition)

When the boss is large enough for all shotgun pellets to connect, this composition guarantees thrilling, hands-on fun.

- **Composition**: Liter / Guilty / Sugar / Drake / Maiden or Isabel

  - **Powerful Close-Range Firepower**: Paired with shotgun buffers like Sugar and Drake, they can unleash unimaginable damage up close.
