## News Summary

According to IT Chosun, platforms like 'Erolabs' are distributing adult games while evading regulations from the Korea Communications Standards Commission (KCSC) and the Game Rating and Administration Committee (GRAC), creating a regulatory blind spot. It has been pointed out that this leaves adult games exposed without proper safeguards.

## My Thoughts

Censoring games is ironic. Overseas, when accessing adult content, users are often simply asked to confirm their age, which could be evidence that the issue is not taken as seriously as it is domestically.

Furthermore, to purchase games on platforms like Steam, a card is required. Considering that it is difficult for minors to obtain a credit card in their own name, this payment process itself can be seen as a practical barrier. In such a situation, censoring harmless content could, paradoxically, prevent many gamers from having diverse experiences.
